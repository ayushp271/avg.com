Project Advisory & Consulting

Ape Vision LLP

Project advisory & consulting

Turning complex projects into informed decisions.

Ape Vision LLP is the consulting and project advisory arm of APE Vision Group, providing specialised technical and commercial support across the energy, infrastructure and industrial sectors.

The company supports developers, investors, EPC contractors, industries and government organisations across the project lifecycle — from early-stage concept development and technical evaluation through execution support.

Pre-Bid Support & Tender Engineering

Helping clients evaluate opportunities, develop technical approaches and prepare for competitive project environments.

02
Feasibility & Techno-Economic Assessment

Evaluating technical viability, commercial fundamentals and project economics before significant resources are committed.

03
Detailed Project Reports & Bankable Reports

Developing structured project documentation to support decision-making, financing and execution.

04
Basic Engineering & Owner's Engineering

Providing technical direction and engineering oversight aligned with project objectives.

05
Project Management Consultancy

Supporting clients through planning, coordination, monitoring and project execution.

06
Technical Due Diligence & Project Advisory

07
Financial Modelling & Cost Optimisation

Connecting technical decisions with commercial outcomes.

Construction Monitoring & Contract Administration

Supporting project owners during implementation to maintain alignment with scope, schedule, cost and contractual requirements.

Ape Vision Techinfra Pvt. Ltd.

Engineering, EPC & infrastructure development

Engineering the infrastructure of tomorrow.

Ape Vision Techinfra Pvt. Ltd. is the flagship engineering and infrastructure company of APE Vision Group, focused on delivering sustainable, technology-driven and future-ready infrastructure solutions across India and international markets.

The company operates through both Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) and Developer / Investor models, enabling it to participate across the project lifecycle — from concept and engineering through construction, commissioning and long-term asset ownership.

Energy Storage & Clean Technologies
