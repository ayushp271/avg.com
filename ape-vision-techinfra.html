About Ape Vision Techinfra

From engineering to execution.

The infrastructure of the future is becoming more complex. Data centres require enormous amounts of power and increasingly sophisticated technical infrastructure. Renewable energy requires integrated generation, transmission and storage. Emerging technologies require new approaches to engineering, project development and execution.

Ape Vision Techinfra exists at this intersection — developing, engineering, procuring, constructing and managing technologically advanced projects across infrastructure and clean-energy sectors.

EPC MODE

Engineering, procurement & construction

Delivering engineering, procurement and construction capabilities for project owners and developers.

DEVELOPER / INVESTOR MODE

Development & investment

Participating in project development and opportunities where the Group can contribute beyond execution.

This dual approach allows Ape Vision Techinfra to participate across different stages of the infrastructure value chain — from project concept and development through execution and long-term asset ownership or support.
